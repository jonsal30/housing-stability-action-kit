Emergency cash: start with the lowest-risk options

Ask family/friends for a short, written zero-interest loan or a truck/driver instead of cash; request a hardship advance from an employer if eligible; ask 211 about verified charitable grants; seek hospital bill financial assistance and any valid accident insurance claim. Ask a local credit union about alternatives only if repayment is realistic. Do not take a high-cost payday/title loan or pledge the only vehicle without understanding the terms.

Rental aid reality check

Some programs require an active tenancy and may not be able to reverse an executed writ. Catholic Charities' city rental application was listed as closed when checked. Ask 211 which funds are accepting cases and whether a signed agreement from the landlord is required.

Transition rule: one call, one box, one photograph. Then mark it done. Progress beats perfect organization.

Boundaries + emotional first aid

60-second grounding: feet down, unclench jaw, slow exhale, water. Name the next physical action. Feeling overwhelmed does not require a long explanation before taking action.

When someone starts crying: "I hear you and I care. We have 12 minutes to secure the essentials. Can we talk about how this feels after the boxes are safe?"

To a parent/helper: "Thank you for helping. I can use a truck/ride and a calm plan right now. I cannot manage criticism while I am packing. Can we focus on one task, then talk later?"

To yourself: "I can be upset and still do the next five-minute task. I do not need to settle the entire situation tonight."

Helper rule: Ask before posting, fundraising, making calls on someone's behalf, or sharing case details. Assign one person to transportation, one to calls, and one to documentation. Consent and privacy come first.

If anyone is in immediate danger call 911. For acute emotional crisis, call or text 988. A crisis line is not a substitute for legal or housing assistance.

Backup and privacy

Important: This public website contains no saved user data on GitHub. Your entries live only in this browser's local storage / IndexedDB. Another device sees a fresh blank copy until you manually import a backup. Browser cleanup, private mode, lost device, or storage eviction can erase data.

Backups may contain photos, full names, addresses, and case details and are NOT encrypted. Keep the JSON in a secure private location, and share it only with consent. Export frequently; verify the file was saved successfully. Never put the exported JSON or photos in a public GitHub repository.

Legal scope

This is an organizing aid, not legal advice or an attorney relationship. An alleged defect in a 24-hour warning does not automatically void the judgment. A money offer or payment plan is not an effective stay without an enforceable agreement/order and actual coordination with the executing officer. Prioritize safety and possessions while seeking counsel.
